How it works

From lecture slide to long-term memory

Three moves, and the source stays attached the whole way.

Library
01

Bring in your material

PowerPoints, PDFs and lecture videos go into your Library. Nothing to retype.

02

Capture, don’t retype

Drag a box around text or a diagram. It becomes a card with a backlink to its slide.

What is spaced repetition?
Reviews timed just before you forget
AgainHardGoodEasy
03

Review at the right moment

An SM-2 schedule brings each card back right before you’d have forgotten it.

Features

Everything a study library needs

AI regeneration

Let Claude rewrite a rough capture into a proper question and answer — or leave it exactly as written. Bring your own key, or use the built-in allowance.

Video, too

Pause a lecture recording, OCR the frame, or mark a transcript range — the timeline remembers where every card came from.

Folders & tags

Nest folders as deep as you like and tag across them.

Card graph

See how cards, folders and source slides connect.

Anki import & export

Bring in an Anki deck, or take yours with you anytime.

Live sessions

Host a quiz from any prepared folder; friends join with a code.
