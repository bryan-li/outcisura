A study library, assembled from what you already read

Flashcards, cut straight from the slide.

Import a PDF or PowerPoint deck. Click the part worth remembering — it becomes a card, editable and AI-sharpened, always one click from the exact slide it came from.

Download for Mac Apple Silicon & Intel · latest release on GitHub
I

Click to capture

Drag a box around any region of a slide — text, a diagram, an equation — and it's staged as a card in seconds.

II

AI regeneration

Let Claude rewrite a rough capture into a proper question and answer, or leave it exactly as written.

III

Video, too

Pause a lecture recording, OCR the frame, or mark a transcript range — the timeline remembers where every card came from.

IV

Spaced repetition

An SM-2 schedule decides what's due, so you meet each card again right before you'd have forgotten it.
